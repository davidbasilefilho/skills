2. Ideal quest role (leader, strategist, healer, scout, builder, trickster)
3. Environment you feel at home in (sea, forest, forge, city, night, sky, battlefield, library)
4. Flaw: your "fatal flaw" (hubris, loyalty, grudge, ambition, impatience…)

6. What you'd sacrifice for a friend
7. Free-time pull (music, crafting, gardening, sport, sleeping, pranks, reading)
