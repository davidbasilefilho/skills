# Progression reference

Source: Supercell's upgrade tables published 10 Nov 2025 (applied 24 Nov 2025), as compiled by third parties in 2026. Balance and economy change often, so verify with a web search when the environment allows, and tell the player the date of the data you used.

## Real level offsets

Real level = API `level` + (16 − API `maxLevel`).

| Rarity | Unlocks at real level | API maxLevel | Offset |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 1 | 16 | +0 |
| Rare | 3 | 14 | +2 |
| Epic | 6 | 11 | +5 |
| Legendary | 9 | 8 | +8 |
| Champion | 11 | 6 | +10 |

## Gold per upgrade step

Below level 12 gold depends on rarity; from 12 up it is the same for every rarity.

| To level | Gold |
|---|---|
| 10 | 8,000 (legendary: 5,000) |
| 11 | 15,000 |
| 12 | 25,000 |
| 13 | 40,000 |
| 14 | 60,000 |
| 15 | 90,000 |
| 16 | 120,000 |

## King Tower

XP was removed (mid-2026). King Tower level rises when enough cards reach a level, e.g. King 14 needs 12 cards at level 13+, King 15 needs 13 cards at 14+, King 16 needs 14 cards at 15+. Tower troops don't count.

## Other 2026 notes

- Card Mastery tasks pay a flat amount of gold per task (4,000 / 6,000 / 15,000), a good gold source for cards the player already uses.
- Elite Wild Cards no longer exist.
